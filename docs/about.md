# About this course

Backend Mastery is a free course. It teaches backend engineering from zero to senior level
by building one app, Bazaar, from a tiny script to a sharded, multi-server system.

## Credits

- The SQL Playground in Module 14 is **inspired by [SQLBolt](https://sqlbolt.com/)**.
  All lessons, tasks and datasets here are original and use Bazaar's data.
- Built with [MkDocs](https://www.mkdocs.org/) and [Material for MkDocs](https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/).

## Your progress

Your progress (finished lessons and quiz scores) is saved only in your browser,
using `localStorage`. Nothing is sent to any server. If you clear your browser data,
your progress is reset.
