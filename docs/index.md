## How this course works

- **One app, one story.** Every lesson, lab and diagram is about Bazaar.
  Each new problem Bazaar faces brings the next topic, so you always know **why** you are learning it.
- **Explained completely.** Every lesson goes from "why does this exist?" to "how does it work inside?",
  answers the questions you are likely to have, and ends with a cheat sheet. You should never need another website.
- **See it move.** Every concept has an interactive diagram. Press Play and watch it happen step by step.
- **Do it yourself.** Every lesson has a lab you run on your own computer with `docker compose up`.
  Each lab checks itself with `test_lab.sh`.
- **Check yourself.** Every lesson ends with a quiz, and every answer is explained.
- **No sign-up.** Your progress is saved in this browser only.
